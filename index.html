2. The core promise: your ledger stays on your phone / 核心承诺：账本只存在你手机里

The App has no account system and no backend server. It never uploads your records. Everything you enter — amounts, dates, categories, notes — is written to the App’s private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences / app-private directory). Uninstalling the App removes that data. When you use Export CSV, a file is written to the App’s own storage area and shared only through the Android system share sheet, if you choose to share it.

本应用没有账号体系，也没有后台服务器，不会把你的记账数据上传到任何地方。 你输入的金额、日期、分类、备注全部写入手机本机应用私有存储（Android SharedPreferences / 应用私有目录）， 卸载即清除。使用「导出 CSV」时，文件写入应用自身存储区，只有你主动分享时才会通过系统分享面板交出去。

3. What the ad SDK collects / 广告 SDK 会收集什么

The App shows advertising provided by Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google’s SDK may process the following on your device:

  • Advertising identifier (GAID / AAID) and device model, OS version, screen metrics, language, manufacturer;
  • Network information including IP address, and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • App-open time, session length, ad requests, impressions, clicks and conversions;
  • Whether you completed a rewarded-video view (used only to unlock the CSV-export feature inside the App).

The App itself does not collect your contacts, photos, messages, location via GPS, call log, or any content of your bookkeeping records, and does not send any of them to Google or to us.

4. Your choices / 你的选择

  • Consent gate. The Google AdMob SDK is not initialized until you tap “同意 / Agree” on the first-launch dialog. If you choose “不同意 / Decline”, the App still works fully and simply shows no ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads. Reset or disable the advertising identifier in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or use Google’s Ads Settings (myadcenter.google.com).
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the App removes all local records. You can also clear them via Android Settings → Apps → Xiaotan Bookkeeping → Storage → Clear data.

同意门控：在你于首次启动弹窗点击「同意」之前，AdMob SDK 不会初始化；选择「不同意」时应用功能完整，只是不展示广告。 个性化广告退出：可在 Android「设置 → Google → 广告」重置或停用广告标识符。删除数据：卸载即清除， 或在「设置 → 应用 → 小谭记账 → 存储 → 清除数据」中清空。

7. Security / 安全

Because records never leave the device, there is no transmission to protect. Data sits in the App’s private sandbox, which Android isolates from other apps. Keep your device unlocked-state in mind: anyone with physical access to an unlocked phone can open the App.

由于数据不出设备，不存在传输环节。数据保存在应用私有沙箱内，Android 会将其与其他应用隔离。 请注意：能物理接触到未锁屏手机的人即可打开本应用。
