2048 Merge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

2048 Merge runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server and no cloud storage. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal information you enter or generate while playing.

With your consent, the app initializes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect or request the device's advertising ID (GAID), approximate location derived from device settings, and app-open / ad-interaction information, in order to serve and measure ads. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can decline ad consent inside the app; the game keeps working fully and simply shows no ads.

Data we do NOT collect

No name, email, phone, contacts, location history, photos, files or gameplay data is collected or sent anywhere by this app.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children.
