Tic Tac Toe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Tic Tac Toe runs entirely on your device. It is a three-in-a-row board game played against the device. It has no account, no server and no cloud storage. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal information you enter or generate while playing.

Advertising

This app is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). With your consent, the app initializes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect or request the device's advertising ID (GAID), approximate location derived from device settings, and app-open / ad-interaction information, in order to serve and measure ads. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can decline ad consent inside the app; the game keeps working fully and simply shows no ads.

Data we do NOT collect

No name, email, phone, contacts, location history, photos, files or gameplay data is collected or sent anywhere by this app.

Children

The app is a general-audience board game and is not directed at children.
